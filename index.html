About this pilot

Illuminated Word is a reading path, not a translation library. Scripture text displayed in full is the King James Version (KJV), which is in the public domain and free to republish. Five further long-loved translations — New King James Version, New Living Translation, The Passion Translation, The Message, and the Amplified Bible — are each under active publisher copyright, so instead of reproducing their text here, each verse page names them as further reading to explore through their publishers. Every book begins with a classical painting and a short, plain-language summary, so you always know what you're about to read before you read it.

Illuminated Word — Scripture text: King James Version (public domain). Book summaries are original editorial content.
