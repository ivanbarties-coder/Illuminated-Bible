Exodus · Chapter 20

Old Testament

20And God spake all these words, saying,

21I am the LORD thy God, which have brought thee out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage.

22Thou shalt have no other gods before me.

23Thou shalt not make unto thee any graven image, or any likeness of any thing that is in heaven above, or that is in the earth beneath, or that is in the water under the earth:

24Thou shalt not bow down thyself to them, nor serve them: for I the LORD thy God am a jealous God, visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children unto the third and fourth generation of them that hate me;

25And shewing mercy unto thousands of them that love me, and keep my commandments.

26Thou shalt not take the name of the LORD thy God in vain; for the LORD will not hold him guiltless that taketh his name in vain.

27Remember the sabbath day, to keep it holy.

28Six days shalt thou labour, and do all thy work:

29But the seventh day is the sabbath of the LORD thy God: in it thou shalt not do any work, thou, nor thy son, nor thy daughter, thy manservant, nor thy maidservant, nor thy cattle, nor thy stranger that is within thy gates:

30For in six days the LORD made heaven and earth, the sea, and all that in them is, and rested the seventh day: wherefore the LORD blessed the sabbath day, and hallowed it.

31Honour thy father and thy mother: that thy days may be long upon the land which the LORD thy God giveth thee.

32Thou shalt not kill.

33Thou shalt not commit adultery.

34Thou shalt not steal.

35Thou shalt not bear false witness against thy neighbour.

36Thou shalt not covet thy neighbour's house, thou shalt not covet thy neighbour's wife, nor his manservant, nor his maidservant, nor his ox, nor his ass, nor any thing that is thy neighbour's.
